# Jumpscare

Turn any creature into a jumpscare monster. When a player's creature enters its range, or the monster
walks into the creature's sight, that player gets a full-screen scare with a custom image or GIF and sound.

## Features

- **Individual or Party mode**: scare only the player who triggered it, or the whole party.
- **Per-player toggle**: choose which players can be jumpscared.
- **Range and shape**: circle or 90° cone for every creature. Normal creatures reveal fog in their range.
- **Custom scares per monster**: your own image or GIF, sound, duration and volume.
- **Extra scare effects**: dark heartbeat build-up, lunge, strobe, glitch, static and a built-in boom.

## How to use

1. Install the extension and turn it on in your room.
2. Click the Jumpscare icon in the toolbar and choose **Open settings** (GM).
3. Add a token, tick **Jumpscare**, set its range, owner, image and sound.
4. Right-click any character token → **Token Settings** to set its range and shape.
5. Each player clicks **Enable sound** once when they join.

## Notes

- Range and sight are measured by distance only. Walls are not taken into account.
- Please warn your players before using jumpscares.
